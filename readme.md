### Background
Computation Fluid Dynamics (CFD) is a powerful commercial tool that supports all varieties of fluid flow simulations. This project includes forced internal flow simulations within fuel coaxial swirl injector manifold used onboard liquid rockets to highlight the capabilities of CFD.

---

### Goal
Successfully operate Star-CCM+ for an internal flow sim on real propulsion hardware. Go through setup theory, including surface/mesh quality checks and mesh & prism layer theory.

Sanity checks include a reasonable dP, continuity and momentum, and reasonable v and T gradients.
